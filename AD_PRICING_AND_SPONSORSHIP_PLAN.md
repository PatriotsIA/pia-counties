## Positioning

The brochure’s core promise is:

> Advertise with impact. Connect with a motivated market that values relationship, community, and action-driven partnership.

The website should sell sponsorships as community support, not just banner ads. Sales language should emphasize:

- Patriot-aligned businesses.
- Local county visibility.
- Community action.
- Preferred partner recognition.
- County and statewide reach.
- Sponsor support for useful civic infrastructure.

Avoid leading with “buy a banner ad.” Lead with “become a founding county sponsor,” “presented by,” and “support your county’s civic hub.”
